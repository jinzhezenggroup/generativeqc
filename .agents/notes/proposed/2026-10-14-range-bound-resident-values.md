# Experiment: bind resident canonical ERIs to their radial operator

Status: provider prototype; no production selection or endpoint claim
Date: 2026-10-14

The default initializer on `22ff59fd3` completes 96-atom cold in 1332.267 s
and five warm calls, then times out in moved/0 at the original 1800-second
whole-point limit. A separate finite short trace observes four VV10 feature
sweeps averaging 81.964 s and six actual Fock evaluations across a failed-seed
retry. Range exchange also recomputes its fixed-geometry ERIs on every Fock.
The canonical geometry-only Schwarz predicate does not become cheaper merely
because an incremental consumer supplies a small density difference.

The existing resident source stores full-range ERIs only. This prototype
extends that same bounded canonical source to one immutable full/SR/LR radial
identity, including omega. An SR/LR lease accepts only matching pure-exchange
requests; it cannot supply full Coulomb, full exchange or another omega.
Preparation invokes the existing compiler-owned scalar source, finite-audits
it before publication and charges selected-bucket descriptors and every value.
Partial residency preserves exact Direct evaluation of uncached buckets, the
original Schwarz predicate, spin normalization, compensation and work census.
Allocation refusal leaves the ordinary provider intact. Derivatives continue
to use the independent derivative owner; cached zero-order values cannot
authorize derivative reuse.

A scheduled metadata-only inventory on the original 96-atom geometry finds
451,133,882 screened canonical values at 48 atoms (3,609,071,056 bytes), and
1,873,221,716 at 96 atoms (14,985,773,728 bytes). These are source-value bytes,
not complete endpoint capacity or a promise that residency fits. Solver, XC,
grid, provider, host preparation, transient and force lifetimes still need
complete admission. The inventory evaluates no ERIs and is not timing evidence.

Independent PySCF SR/LR matrices, signed RKS/UKS densities, full/partial leases,
changed geometry, immutable identity and bounded-refusal tests must pass before
connecting this provider to an experimental KS policy. Then require complete
molecular E/F and original endpoint populations before any automatic selection.
This work does not close #2426 or justify changing its deadline or gates.
