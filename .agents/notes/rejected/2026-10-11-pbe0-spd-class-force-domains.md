## Why W is not the next first priority

CUDA KS stages resident orbital-energy W and the shared KS validator also
constructs a host W. The HF weighted callback is explicitly a different DFD
contract and must not be substituted. A correct resident download would need
authenticated orbital inputs, per-spin downloads before UKS overwrites alpha
with total W, explicit column/row-major conversion, unchanged published leases,
and honest additional movement accounting.

## Proposed existing-provider admission

The current `direct_bounded_fallback.cu` admits immutable class-major force
domains only when the whole basis has maximum shell angular momentum three.
Pure s/p/d bases instead use the combined low partition plus existing order-seven
and order-eight consumers. The class-page decoder already assigns all 21 s/p/d
quartet classes to disjoint low/order-four/order-five/cooperative/materialized
domains, without retained descriptors or another full candidate scan.

Investigate admitting maximum-two bases to that same owner when the same
coherent topology, compatible resident primitive cache, both existing derivative
algebras and normal launch dimensions are present. Keep maximum-zero/one and
unproved/custom/missing-cache owners on their current fallback initially. Pure
s/p/d has no f-containing residual, so do not launch the f-only consumer there.
Mixed f keeps exactly its existing residual launch, predicates and error handling.

This follows GPU4PySCF's class-specific work-scheme direction without replacing
our integral mathematics by Rys. No screening relaxation, direct-to-DF change,
incremental Fock substitution, recurrence or derivative truncation is proposed.

## Qualification required before performance publication

- Exhaustive host class/page ownership, empty/multi-system inventories, both
  source layouts/spins and submission/reset failure tests.
- Independent two-electron derivative gates with arbitrary/opposing/zero
  densities, unequal primitive lengths, Cartesian/spherical bases and geometries;
  retained missing-cache/custom-dimension fallback checks.
- Real GPU safety and diagnostics only through finite Slurm on allowed nodes,
  preserving assigned visibility and verified compiler-cache provenance.
- Source-matched complete PBE0 energy+force at 48/96 atoms, original/moved,
  frozen owner-local density, all slow/failed samples, unchanged E/F gates and
  available semantic work. Capacity counts must not become executed work claims.
- Five interleaved pairs and unchanged robust 2%/relative-MAD acceptance before
  a stage PR. A small pilot is only a next-action gate, not formal promotion.
