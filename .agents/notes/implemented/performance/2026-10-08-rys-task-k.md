# Decision: independent lane-local Direct Rys-K

Status: implemented (opt-in; not promoted to the default)
Date: 2026-10-08

The opt-in decision below is preserved as measured. Default selection is
superseded by [the qualified five-class promotion](2026-10-08-rys-task-k-default.md),
which adds separate unset-selector endpoint qualification.

## Problem

Replacing the incumbent value recurrence with component-lane Rys did not copy
the scheduling/contraction engine of a specialized Rys-K implementation. For
small Cartesian blocks, cooperative root preparation and per-primitive
barriers can cost more than the integral arithmetic. Identical admitted
quartet counts alone do not establish identical primitive/root work.

The implementation and evidence use frozen base
`4385f72751b829883407c01106186917c344317b`, including the existing primitive-pair
cache, K density bounds, default cross-chunk filling and default bounded MD-J.
The original working checkout has unrelated changes and a different history;
this experiment lives in the detached worktree
`/data/jzzeng/qc-rys-task-k-20261008`. No production-default or SCF-tolerance
change is needed to obtain the measured result.

## Decision

Add independent value-only `_rys_task` AOT variants and
`GENERATIVEQC_DIRECT_K_FOCK_LOWERING=rys-task`. A packed 32-thread CTA owns 32
quartets, rather than assigning Cartesian components of one quartet to its
lanes. Only the existing admission/launch plumbing synchronizes. Geometry,
roots, TRR/HRR values and integral accumulators stay lane-local through each
primitive product. The existing Rys axis program owns all recurrence algebra;
this is not a second ERI formula implementation.

Restrict capability to one/two roots, s/p/d, fourth center <=p and <=27 Cartesian
components. Intersect the incumbent compiled streaming inventory. Restricted
raw K contracts into bounded local blocks before atomic publication; other
spin/consumer semantics retain the generic scatter. The candidate's 64-double
block bound is explicit and does not alter the incumbent block experiment's
32-double bound. Pair caching, queue filling and density/Schwarz admission are
not reimplemented.

Prepared K selection is independent of J and force selection. Unsupported or
unselected classes retain the incumbent. Optional storage failure retains the
existing bounded provider fallback; launch errors are not retried into an
already-written output. Portable profiles compile no new candidate inventory.
No method/molecule/density-sensitive promotion table is introduced.

## Rejected alternatives

- Re-enabling the old cooperative `rys` lowering: the final fixed-density
  96-atom qualification takes approximately 4.37 s versus 1.68 s incumbent,
  despite identical 81,907,624 admitted shell quartets. The inventories differ,
  so this is not a primitive/root-work equivalence proof.
- Initial lane-local V1 with a non-inlined two-root evaluator and a 32-double
  block bound: controlled Cold medians show only about 1.2% advantage, but the
  candidate mean regresses and Fock counts fluctuate (17--22). Do not promote
  from that median alone.
- V2 full eight-class selection: two-root inlining and a 64-double block bound
  improve fixed-density K by about 10.5%, but the feasibility Cold endpoint
  regresses 113.55/17 Focks to 129.53/21 Focks. Kernel wins cannot replace
  complete-endpoint evidence.
- Unconditionally replacing `ssss` and `ppss`: diagnostic Nsight Systems
  reports regressions. Two identical diagnostic K calls total 141.047 to
  189.428 ms for `ssss` and 179.781 to 212.856 ms for `ppss`. `psss`'s gain is
  small. Retain these incumbents in the measured explicit selection.
- Treating this as atomic elimination or an occupancy optimization: K still
  uses atomic publication, and several candidate kernels need 253--254
  registers. Zero streaming stack/spills is not an occupancy guarantee.

The measured selection is explicitly
`psps,ppps,dsss,dpss,dsps`, via
`GENERATIVEQC_AOT_RYS_TASK_FOCK_SHELL_CLASSES`. Unset/`all` still means capability,
not a performance winner. This selector is intersected with enabled incumbent
coverage and frozen at preparation.

## Invariants and identity

- Strict FP64 remains the only changed-path precision; mathematical root count
  follows the value IR, not the force IR or a shell-name numerical rule.
- The one-root rule uses the existing Boys owner (F0 weight, squared node F1/F0);
  the two-root path inlines the existing regenerated high-accuracy tables.
- Preserve AO normalization, ordering, pair orientation, symmetry and RHF/UHF
  scatter. No oracle/reference evaluation participates in production.
- Independent frozen-base regeneration matches the previous full-bundle golden
  fixture. Incumbent, old Rys-value and old K-block profile source hashes match
  the candidate byte-for-byte for both sm_120 and portable sm_90.
- Bundle identity changes only to include the new variant (including an empty
  portable variant comment) and registry mask/launcher/filter. Manifest/catalog
  and public generator ABI remain unchanged. The fixture now separately pins
  retained source bytes, in addition to current full-bundle fingerprints.

## Evidence

The first final holdout is ten independent fresh processes in Slurm job 6644,
same library/device/protocol, with five alternating pairs and every repeat
retained. PBE0 RKS, water-96, spherical def2-SVP/768 AOs, 48x16x32 grid, screening
1e-12, energy tolerance 1e-12, density tolerance 1e-10, max 100 iterations.
Persistent compiler/artifact caches are shared equally; no CUDA context,
density or prepared owner is primed. Construction, preparation, the first SCF,
analytic forces, host force return and owner teardown are timed.

- Incumbent seconds: 112.537945, 112.729466, 122.431218, 122.345066, 113.301060.
- Candidate seconds: 109.151120, 109.265324, 109.240038, 109.259336, 109.245257.
- Complete medians: 113.301060 to 109.245257 s, 1.037126x / 3.58% less time.
- Actual Fock counts: incumbent 17,17,19,19,17; candidate 17,17,17,17,17.
- GPU4PySCF independent endpoint reference: maximum energy error
  9.09e-12 Ha and maximum force error 3.79e-11 Ha/Bohr, below 1e-8 / 1e-7 gates.
- The complete aggregate includes both 19-Fock controls. The 17-Fock control
  subset also beats its candidate counterpart by about 3.1%; this is diagnostic
  attribution, not a reason to discard any endpoint sample.

Standalone Libcint gates pass all 384 matrices: eight classes, three geometries
(unequal/signed primitives, coincident and reversed pairs), both spins, four
consumer semantics, one task and a 33-task warp tail. Memcheck/synccheck report
zero errors and racecheck reports zero hazards/errors/warnings, each on 64
selected tests in finite Slurm job 6646. Integrated six-atom public and prepared
SCF raw-K gates pass 48 inputs, both spins, nonsymmetric/symmetric densities,
full/scaled/zero/restored inputs, all/five/none selections and environment
mutation after preparation; maximum matrix error is 6.37e-12.

An earlier attempted holdout is invalid: its selector build failed, and its
driver continued with a stale library. It is quarantined under
`results/invalid-stale-selector/`, not used in acceptance. The corrected build
is fail-closed; binary preflight verifies masks 1236/1247/0, and every worker
checks the frozen library SHA256. The accepted binary is
`7fe0cec3a72ceed6d35c65da69b909d30ca19a5e67dfc12e3cbb70b0189de030`.

Additional final fixed-density, 48-atom and independent repeat-cohort evidence
is retained in the [benchmark receipt](../../../../benchmarks/results/rys-task-cold-20261008/README.md).
Diagnostic profiler times are not added to complete endpoint timings.

Independent confirmation (job 6653, three processes per mode) passes: medians
113.489543 to 109.482571 s, 3.53% less complete time; Focks 17,17,19 versus
17,17,17. The 48-atom complete holdout passes 51.198094 to 48.740725 s, 4.80%
less time, with 19 Focks in every sample. All three cohort means also improve.
The driver now gates on both means and medians, rejecting V1-like regressions
that a median alone can hide. The final five-class fixed-density 96 K median
is 1.683338 to 1.456879 s (13.45% less time), not the full-inventory V2 result.

NCU cannot collect counters (`ERR_NVGPUCTRPERM`); no active-lane or barrier-stall
fraction is claimed. Nsight Systems and source structure support the class
selection, but do not replace that missing mechanism evidence. Final formatter
cleanup changes a Python constant's layout only: regenerated CUDA/registry
golden hashes remain identical, and the exact qualified compiler/native input
capsule is retained alongside all numerical/endpoint observations.

## Consequences and revisit conditions

Keep the explicit selection and incumbent rollback. A measured benefit on this
case is not a general Rys-over-Hermite claim or permission to promote all eight
classes. Revisit default promotion only with complete endpoint and work counts
across methods, geometries, bases and sizes, including independent numerical
gates and register/local/shared-memory constraints. Future tuning may change
the bounded class/subgroup mapping while preserving the same algebra, queue
and prepared lifetime owners.
