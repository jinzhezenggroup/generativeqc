Status: implemented; component-qualified, complete-endpoint qualification pending

## Decision

Hold D's physical owner at the common origin and coalesce the constant owner
incidence in pair-factor derivatives, Coulomb coordinate scales and Gaussian
prefactor derivatives before composing factor products. The existing canonical
factor algebra and scalar Graph/AD still own all mathematical derivatives and
product rules. The generated leaf has three, six or nine independent coordinates
and nine, 36 or 81 ordered second partials. The all-distinct operation remains
explicit, while all-one-owner quartets retain the scalar leaf.

Owner identity comes from topology, never coordinate equality. Non-origin owner
labels follow first occurrence; D's owner uses the final label. Equal labels
must have identical coordinates. Distinct coincident owners remain independent
and can have nonzero relative curvature. The ordered exhaustive consumer keeps
all nine mathematical coordinates even when owners coincide, providing a
separate end-to-end comparison for the symmetry-unique consumer.

The private matrix API and all callers migrate together without an obsolete
alias. Primitive output is stack-local with 13, 43 or 91 fields. Compensated
physical/origin scatter and existing matrix staging remain unchanged.
