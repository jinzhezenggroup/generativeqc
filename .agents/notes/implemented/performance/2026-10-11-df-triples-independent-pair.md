# Decision: group independent prepared W seeds, not their scientific algebra

Status: implemented (qualified patch for an independent PR)
Date: 2026-10-11

## Problem

After distinct occupied moments and bounded panel traversal, each swapped W seed
still submits both original products separately. Qualified micro and actual-owner
prototype measurements identify dispatch/audit overhead, not a new lower-order
equation. Their history and rejected alternatives remain in
`../../proposed/2026-10-11-df-triples-pointer-batched-w.md`; prototype timing does
not qualify the canonical implementation.

## Decision

The shared prepared backend applies one existing ordinary strict-FP64 descriptor
to two independent sets of borrowed views. CUDA address publication and vendor
dispatch belong there, not in compiler glue or the native CC method. The compiler
derives the two paired W bindings from the original TensorIR and demands exact
descriptor/physical-leading-dimension equality without adding a descriptor.

The native energy owner groups only swapped seeds with adjacent canonical cubes
inside the actual three-panel strict-FP64 pedantic domain. It admits a 48-byte
stream-exclusive pointer table (+256 aligned arena bytes) after the original
panel/provider choices. Budget refusal retains the original choices. Typed device
allocation refusal resets an in-place optional Context and prepares the original
layout before input transfers or scientific kernel enqueues. Other errors propagate. No execution
failure retries a partially evaluated seed.

## Invariants

- Original two products per seed, alpha/beta, physical views, per-seed order,
  publication precision, semantic summands and every finite-audit value remain.
- Original canonical energy addresses, reduction order, panel LRU, six moment
  cubes, force/response behavior and public/result ABI remain.
- Pair outputs are mutually disjoint and disjoint from all four inputs, scratch
  and arithmetic state. Shared read-only inputs are legal. Scratch/counter/
  capture/context/alias refusals precede the address binder.
- Checked, scaled, batched, mixed, generated and CuMetal recipes remain ordinary.
  Padded/nonadjacent API outputs retain two affine scans; only adjacent dense
  outputs share a scan. Actual driver calls are not semantic products or a count
  of vendor-internal kernels.
- Group collection has six entries: non-FP64 all-equal tiles still need all six
  independent seeds, even though eligible strict groups have at most two.
