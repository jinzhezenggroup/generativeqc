# Decision: lower sparse Cartesian-axis products through existing scalar AD

Status: implemented; production component-qualified, complete-endpoint qualification pending
Date: 2026-11-14

Subsequent complete frozen-integral qualification with independently revalidated
nonzero native W is recorded in
[the saved-determinant decision](../numerics/2026-11-14-revalidate-native-saved-determinants.md).
That later component does not upgrade the explicitly zero-W prefixes below to
physical Hessian evidence or close complete-endpoint qualification.

## Problem

The bounded actual frozen-matrix diagnosis recorded in the preceding proposal
identified matrix primitives separately from scalar calls and other consumer
work. Three dense axis jets were expanded despite each coefficient having
derivative support on just its own Cartesian axis. Source appearance alone did
not prove machine-code overhead, so a private candidate preceded this change.

## Decision

Keep the incumbent left-associated three-factor scalar product in Graph. Seed
each coefficient's derivatives only for its supported Cartesian axis, then use
the existing Graph differentiation and scalar emitter to lower its value,
gradient and every ordered mixed partial. Materialize the dense physical-owner
jet only for the unchanged Coulomb product. The coefficient dimension can be
four when there are three non-origin owners; its matrix stride is independent
of the physical coordinate dimension.

Pair coefficients, Coulomb states, Gaussian prefactors, geometry, owner
incidence, scalar/first/mixed operations and native consumers retain their
existing authorities. Numerical zeros remain ordinary leaves. No second
handwritten Hessian algebra, transpose copy, screening, persistent cache,
resident-storage requirement or production reference engine is introduced.
The source identity intentionally changes because the emitted schedule changes.
