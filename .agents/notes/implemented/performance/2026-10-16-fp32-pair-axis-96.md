small-system native-force ownership result. The incumbent canonical mixed J/K
source still prepares the three independent Hermite axes serially outside the
already-qualified order-three schedule. A trial lets lanes zero through two

primitive geometry. Existing publication/retirement barriers, complete workspace

and legacy packet scheduling retain their original specialization.

functional-name optimization. The prior strict order-three rationale remains
in [the implemented decision](2026-10-11-order-three-axis-and-lr-force-scheduling.md).
