# Decision: Extend the CPU saddle lease to qualified scalar ECP protocols

Status: implemented
Date: 2026-11-24

The existing Cs46 and I28 saddle selectors keep their operator, root and step
requirements. Within that adapter, the native lease authenticates the linked
Hessian ECP system and reserves the existing conservative setup inventory.
The stationary Direct force path and admission share one integral-gradient
capacity query, including the chosen ECP contraction. Do not add a second
stationary force reservation: the fleet already reserves its full source cap.

Both original normalized system handles and the expanded basis retain ECP
terms independently of the batch. Source-metric restore also retains the raw
source's ECP value matrix and may construct its provider on the accepted seed
geometry. Account for those copies without changing restore or SCF equations.
The general SDK force-reservation flag and unqualified ECP selectors are not
expanded by this saddle-specific lease.
